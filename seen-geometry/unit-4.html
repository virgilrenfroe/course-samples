Unit 4 · A factor, and a center · ~35 minutes

Scale — grow / shrink

Factor 2 means twice as far from the center you named. The center is part of the rule. Forget it, and the square also slides.

3 objectives Two centers 1 homework

What you'll do

In this sitting:

  1. Double a square from the origin, and double it again from its own center.
  2. Say what a scale factor does to a distance, and what it does to the center.
  3. Tell a grow-from-zero (the shape also moves out) from a grow-in-place.

Lesson

Distances from a center

A scale multiplies distances from a center you name. Factor 2: twice as far from that center. Factor 1/2: half as far. Factor 1: no change. This page stays with positive factors. A negative factor would also flip the shape through the center. We will not build a unit on flips. If you need a flip later, say “flip” and show the before and after. Do not hide it inside the word “scale.”

The square starts with corners (1, 1), (2, 1), (2, 2), and (1, 2). Side length 1. The open dot is the center, at (1.5, 1.5). The red dot is the corner that starts at (1, 1).

About zero: (x, y) → (kx, ky). About a center C: C + k (P − C). For k = 2 from zero, (1, 1) lands at (2, 2). The side was 1; it becomes 2.

Doubling from zero also slides the center

Press Double from zero. Every coordinate doubles. The side becomes 2. The open dot moves from (1.5, 1.5) to (3, 3). The square grew, and it also moved away from zero, because its center was not zero. People call that “just a scale.” Part of what you see is a slide of the center. Say so.

Press Double from the center (it starts over from the original square, so you can compare). The side is again 2. The open dot stays at (1.5, 1.5). The red corner moves from (1, 1) to (0.5, 0.5): it got farther from the center, not farther from zero in the same way. Same factor. Different center. Different picture.

Half from zero sends (1, 1) to (0.5, 0.5). The side becomes 1/2. The center moves in, to (0.75, 0.75). Shrink toward zero is not the same as shrink in place. Each button replaces the picture. They do not stack. Stacking factors is a composition; Unit 7 stacks a slide and a turn, which is enough for this 101.

Homework

Write it. If someone is running a section, turn it in there. 180–260 words.

Use the square on this page, or a drawing on paper you can measure with a ruler you already have. Double something and say the center. Report: the factor, the side before and after, and whether the center moved. One sentence on what would change your mind (a side that is not twice, or a center that moved when you said it stayed). Do not invent a printed diagram from a class. Do not describe how to build a zoom lens.
