Unit 2 · The same shift · ~40 minutes

Translation — slide in space

Every corner moves by the same amount. The square does not turn. It does not grow.

3 objectives Slide + check 1 homework

What you'll do

In this sitting:

  1. Slide a square on a grid and read the marked corner before and after.
  2. State the rule: (x, y) goes to (x + a, y + b), with the same a and b for every point.
  3. Refuse a sentence that calls a slide a turn or a grow.

Lesson

Same shift, every point

A translation is a slide. Pick a direction and a distance. Every point on the shape moves that far, that way. A book pushed 4 centimeters along the table edge: every corner moved 4 centimeters that way. The book did not spin. The cover did not get larger. If one corner moved farther than another, something else happened — a turn, a bend, a bad reading.

On the grid below, one unit is one square. The pale square starts with its marked corner (the red dot) at (1, 1). The other corners are (2, 1), (2, 2), and (1, 2). Side length: 1 unit. Right means +x. Up means +y. Those are the axes for this page. A button adds or subtracts 1 from a and from b. It does not redraw a new shape. It moves this one.

Translation (x, y) → (x + a, y + b). Example: a = 3, b = 0 sends (1, 1) to (4, 1). Same a and b for every corner.

Teaching grid, drawn for this sample. Not a measurement from a class.

What does not change

Side lengths stay. Angles inside the square stay. The gap between two corners stays the same vector. That is the whole test. If the marked corner goes from (1, 1) to (4, 1), a is 3 and b is 0. The opposite corner goes from (2, 2) to (5, 2). Still a shift of (3, 0). People say “it moved to the right, so it got bigger” because 4 is a bigger number than 1. The address changed. The size did not. Size is the distance between corners, not the size of the address.

You can write that shift as a little table later. You do not need the table to see the slide. The buttons are the lesson. If a button is disabled, the square would leave the grid. That is the page protecting the picture, not a law of nature.

Practice

Which sentence is the slide?

The marked corner started at (1, 1) and is now at (4, 1). The side is still 1 unit long.

Practice

A shift you can do by hand

A corner at (2, 5) is translated by a = −1, b = 2. Where does it land? Type it as x, y.

Homework

Write it. If someone is running a section, turn it in there. 180–280 words. Stay seated.

Slide one object already on a table (coaster, book, phone) in a straight push. Do not turn it on purpose. Name your zero and your unit. Write the marked corner before and after as two pairs of numbers. State a and b. Say one thing that did not change (a side, or the facing of the cover). Then one sentence: what a ruler would have to show before you dropped a or b. If you only used the square on this page, say so, and report the button shifts you actually pressed. Do not invent a lab table. Do not call a slide a rotation.
