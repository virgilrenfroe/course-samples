Unit 5 · Same mesh, many transforms · ~40 minutes

One shape, many matrices

One triangle, five times, on this page. Then a live field of boxes: one mesh, a matrix on each copy.

3 objectives Field + check 1 homework

What you'll do

In this sitting:

  1. Point at copies of one shape and say what they share (the mesh) and what differs (the transform).
  2. Watch 48 copies of one box on this page: one mesh, a matrix on each copy.
  3. Refuse “those are different models” and refuse a homework that counts the boxes.

Lesson

The mesh is the shape list

A mesh, here, is the list of corners that makes one shape. A matrix is the table that places one copy: a slide, a turn, a size, possibly stacked. Five copies can share one list. Each copy gets its own matrix. You do not build the triangle five times. You build it once and you aim it five ways.

The row below is the idea with a number you can count on one hand. One triangle. Five matrices. Invented for this page. Not a count from a class.

Same three corners in the list. Different place, turn, or size. That is one mesh and five matrices.

Forty-eight copies, one box

The view below draws 48 copies of one box. Same corner list. Each copy has its own place, turn, and size. That table of numbers is the matrix. Forty-eight is the number this page chose so the picture stays light. It is not a count from a class. Do not inventory them for the grade.

A few copies drift and turn, so you can see a transform without dragging. Pause that if you want the field still. Phones use the same button. Nothing else on this page has to be dragged.

Practice

What do two copies still share?

Copy A is slid and turned. Copy B is only larger. Both are the box.

Homework

Write it. If someone is running a section, turn it in there. 160–240 words.

Describe the field in four sentences. (1) What is the mesh — the shape that is shared. (2) Name two copies that differ, and say the difference as a slide, a turn, or a size. You may use the five triangles or the box field. (3) Say why “each copy is a different model” is the wrong sentence. (4) What would change your mind: what you would have to see before you believed each copy was a separately built shape. Do not count the boxes. Do not invent a class. If the solid view did not start, say so and use the five triangles.
